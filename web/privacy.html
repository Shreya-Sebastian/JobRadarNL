Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

{{SITE_NAME}} works without an account. If you do not log in, your profile and saved jobs are kept only in your own browser (local storage) and nothing about you is stored on the server. CV text you paste to detect skills is processed once and not stored.

If you create an account

WhatWhyHow long
Your e-mail addressTo send you login links and to recognise your accountUntil you delete the account, or two years after your last login
Your profile (roles, levels, cities, skills and similar preferences) and saved job IDsTo show the same profile on every device you log in onSame as above
Login links and the IP address a link was requested fromTo make links single-use and to stop abuse of the login formTwo days
A session cookie (radar_session)To keep you logged in30 days, or until you log out

Only a one-way hash of each login link and session token is stored, never the token itself. There are no passwords. The legal basis is performance of the service you ask for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). The session cookie is strictly necessary, so it needs no cookie consent. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

On the My profile tab you can download everything stored about you as a JSON file, and delete your account. Deleting removes your e-mail address, profile, saved jobs and sessions from the server straight away. You can also ask for correction or deletion, or object, through the contact below, and you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Who processes it

{{SITE_NAME}} is run by Shreya Sebastian, Eindhoven, the Netherlands. The data is stored on servers in the European Union. Login e-mails are sent through an e-mail delivery service acting as processor. Data is not sold or shared with employers or recruiters.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Shreya-Sebastian/JobRadarNL. For a request about your own data, say that you want to be contacted privately and do not post your e-mail address in the issue.
