        What this radar covers, honestly

        Postings are read from employers' own career sites and from the public boards of nine applicant-tracking platforms, plus AcademicTransfer for universities and Werken voor Nederland for the national government. Only postings located in the Netherlands and classified as tech roles are counted. Every listing links to the employer's original page.

        Not here yet: employers on career-site platforms without an adapter (SAP SuccessFactors is partly covered, Radancy, Phenom, iCIMS are not), most big-tech companies' own job APIs, and anything that only exists on LinkedIn.
